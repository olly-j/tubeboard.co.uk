Independent and private

TubeBoard needs no account and contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking. Favourite stations, recent stations, display choices and Nearby Station matching stay on your device.

Live Activities and opt-in disruption alerts use the routing and service information needed to deliver them. Shared train links contain public train context, not your location or saved stations. The Privacy Policy explains each optional service and its controls.

Questions

A few useful answers.

Is TubeBoard an official TfL app?

No. TubeBoard is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Transport for London.

Does TubeBoard use live data?

Yes. It uses TfL Open Data for live arrival predictions and line status information where available. Predictions can change during disruption or when source data is delayed.

What can I use for free?

The live board, including London Overground departures, station search, automatic refresh, Simple and Detailed modes, five successful Nearby Station activations, Recents, one favourite and three Live Activity sessions are free.

What does Premium add?

Premium unlocks Follow a Train, Next departures and By destination layouts, unlimited favourites, Nearby and Live Activities, station/disruption widgets, Time to Leave and catchability guidance, selected-line disruption alerts, the Apple Watch app and widgets, and colour-by-line boards. Existing purchases include the new Premium features.

Why can a route be visible without a train marker?

Route context and live train information are separate. When TfL does not provide a reliable match, TubeBoard keeps the useful route and pauses unavailable live details. It does not invent a position or countdown.

Does sharing a train share my location?

No. The short-lived link carries public train context, not your precise location or saved stations. A compatible app opens the shared context; the web fallback explains the next step. Browser live tracking is not available.

Does it plan journeys?

No. TubeBoard is designed for the moment when you already know your station and want the next departures quickly.

Where can I download it?

Download TubeBoard from the App Store on an iPhone or iPad running iOS 18.6 or iPadOS 18.6 or later. Premium also includes the companion app for Apple Watch; the native Apple Vision Pro app requires visionOS 26.0 or later.
