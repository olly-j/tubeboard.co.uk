Read it your way

A quick glance, or the detail behind it.

Simple mode keeps the board pared back to destinations and times. Detailed mode adds the line, train identifier and live location where TfL supplies them. Both modes are included for free.

Boards refresh automatically about once a minute. Pull down whenever you want to request the latest predictions.

Independent and private

Useful information, with very little asked of you.

TubeBoard needs no account and contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking. Favourite stations, recent stations, display choices and Nearby Station matching stay on your device.

Live Activities use a short-lived registration so the TubeBoard service can send the selected board through Apple Push Notification service. Inactive registrations are removed automatically.

Questions

A few useful answers.

Is TubeBoard an official TfL app?

No. TubeBoard is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Transport for London.

Does TubeBoard use live data?

Yes. It uses TfL Open Data for live arrival predictions and line status information where available. Predictions can change during disruption or when source data is delayed.

What can I use for free?

The live board, station search, automatic refresh, Simple and Detailed modes, Nearby Station, Recents, one favourite and three Live Activity sessions are free.

What does Premium add?

Premium unlocks unlimited favourites and Live Activities, widgets, Time to Leave and platform headers coloured by Tube line.

Does it plan journeys?

No. TubeBoard is designed for the moment when you already know your station and want the next departures quickly.

When can I download it?

TubeBoard is preparing for its App Store release. This page will link directly to the listing as soon as it is available.
